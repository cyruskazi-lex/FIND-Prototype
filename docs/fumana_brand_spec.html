One brand, one source of truth. Every screen reads from this page.
Fumana means find, in Xhosa and Sesotho. The identity is a clearing house: institutional, precise, settled. Color earns its place by meaning something, type carries the weight, and numbers carry the trust. Hold this and the marketing site, the candidate portal, the employer portal, and Nexus all read as one company.

Scale and surfaces

SPACING . 8pt grid
4 . 8 . 12 . 16 . 24 . 32 . 48. Use these steps only. Consistent rhythm is half of looking expensive.
RADIUS
12 for cards. 8 for inputs. 4 for chips. 0 for rules. Nothing rounder.
ELEVATION
Hairline rules, not drop shadows. A 1px slate line reads as a ledger. Shadows read as generic SaaS. Reserve one soft shadow for overlays only.
DENSITY
Real whitespace. Let figures and rules do the work. Calm is the luxury signal.

Voice
