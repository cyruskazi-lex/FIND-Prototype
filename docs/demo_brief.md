# Fumana demo brief

The front door to this build. Read this first. It explains what the demo is, the one moment that makes it land, and where everything else lives.

Powered by Telos. Designed by Lexington Advisory Group.

---

## What Fumana is

Fumana is a talent clearing house for African engineering. It connects verified African builders to global enterprise clients, and it treats that talent as a settled asset, not aid. The intelligence layer is Telos, a reasoning system licensed from Lexington Advisory Group. The brand is a clearing house: institutional, precise, built so capital and enterprise legal teams trust the numbers on sight.

## What the demo proves

That Fumana verifies talent and shows its reasoning, where a keyword job board cannot. The demo is one seeded scenario told as a single continuous flow across four beats. One builder, one employer, one country.

The scenario: a backend engineer in Lagos, an employer in Berlin needing a payments reconciliation service, and the Nigerian economics that make the impact math real.

## The four beats

1. **Builder.** A messy, informal account of real work becomes a verified, recruiter-ready profile. The Experience Alchemist does the transformation and marks plainly what it will not claim.
2. **Telos match.** Telos shows why this builder fits this employer as a chain of causal factors, not a keyword hit. Zuri narrates the reasoning on demand.
3. **Employer.** The employer sees a bias-shielded, identity-masked profile and an auto-generated Statement of Work, with Fumana as the legal Employer of Record.
4. **EESG impact.** The placement produces an SROI figure that shows its own derivation, mapped to SDG 8, 9, and 17. Trigger a currency move and two agents re-derive the value live.

## The wow moment

Beat 4. Every impact number shows its own math on the same screen, then re-derives in real time when a currency move hits. A sophisticated investor trusts math they can inspect far more than a headline they cannot. No competitor can copy this, because no competitor has the reasoning to back it.

## The two honest lines every viewer should know

- **The figures are computed, never generated.** Impact and financial numbers are calculated in code from visible inputs. The model narrates reasoning only. This is what lets the EESG layer survive scrutiny.
- **The match score is seeded.** The real Bayesian CCM engine does not exist yet. The confidence figure is a demo value, labelled as such on screen. Everything else, the language output, is live.

## What is live

Four live model calls: the Experience Alchemist, Zuri, the Statement of Work, and the two-agent re-derivation. Each uses the same call mechanism. If one works, they all work.

## What this demo is not

Not the platform. No auth, no payments, no admin portal, no real CCM engine, no graph database. Those are documented for later. The demo's only job is to make Telos feel real to a technical or investor audience in under two minutes.
